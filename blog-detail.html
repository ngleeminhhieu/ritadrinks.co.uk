Mango beverage with nata de coco: how wide-opening cans are winning on-the-go shoppers

  • 28 Sep 2026
  • RITA Beverage
Table of contents

    Grab-and-go shoppers have changed what they expect from a canned beverage. A quick hit of sweetness is no longer enough: they want texture, real fruit and a can that is easy to sip from while walking, driving or working. Our Bici bici mango beverage with nata de coco answers all three, and it is quickly becoming one of the most requested formats from importers in the Gulf, Europe and the UK.

    Key takeaways

    • Wide-opening cans make beverages with pieces, such as nata de coco, easy to enjoy without a straw or a spoon.
    • Mango is a familiar, crowd-pleasing flavour that helps a textured beverage win first-time buyers.
    • Convenience stores, petrol forecourts and campus shops are the strongest channels for the format.
    • RITA produces the product under your own label, with free samples, free label design and flexible MOQ.

    Why on-the-go shoppers want more than refreshment

    Ready-to-drink beverages are now bought as a small treat as much as for refreshment. Shoppers who pick up a can on the way to work or the gym want something that feels like a snack and a refreshment in one. Beverages with chewy pieces, from bubble tea to aloe vera with pulp, have grown fast for exactly this reason.

    What a wide-opening can changes

    A standard can opening is designed for liquids. Add pieces and the last few sips get stuck at the bottom, which is a quick way to lose a repeat customer. A wide-opening end lets every cube of nata de coco flow with the liquid, so the last sip is as good as the first. It also gives a fuller aroma of fruit as soon as the can is opened.

    Mango and nata de coco: a pairing built for texture

    Ripe mango is one of the most widely loved fruit flavours in the world, which makes it the ideal entry point for shoppers trying a textured beverage for the first time. Nata de coco, a natural coconut jelly, adds a soft, chewy bite without overpowering the fruit.

    The result is a beverage that works chilled straight from the fridge or poured over ice, and that appeals to both younger shoppers looking for something new and families buying a lunchbox treat.

    Where the format sells best

    Textured beverages perform best where shoppers decide on impulse and open the can straight away. These are the channels our importers report the strongest results from:

    From mango orchard to can: RITA’s supply chain

    Our factory in Vietnam sits close to some of Southeast Asia’s best mango growing regions. Fruit is selected at peak ripeness, processed into purée and blended with nata de coco made in-house, then filled on our canning lines with the same day’s production.

    Quality and certification

    Every batch is checked for sugar level, acidity and piece distribution before it leaves the line. Our production is certified to FSSC 22000, BRCGS, HACCP, ISO 22000 and HALAL standards, so the paperwork your retailers ask for is ready from day one.

    Launching under your own label

    Most of our partners launch the product as their own brand. A typical project follows five steps:

    1. Tell us your market, target price and preferred sweetness level.
    2. Receive free samples to taste and share with your buyers.
    3. Approve a label designed for you free of charge by our in-house team.
    4. Confirm your order with a flexible MOQ that suits a first launch.
    5. Have your goods ready to ship 20–25 days after the order is confirmed.

    “A good textured beverage sells the second can, not just the first. Getting the piece size and the opening right is what keeps shoppers coming back.”

    Marketing ideas that move the can off the shelf

    • Show the texture: use a clear glass and ice in your photos so shoppers can see the pieces.
    • Lead with the fruit: “real mango” on the front of the can does more work than any claim on the back.
    • Sample in store: textured beverages convert best once people have tasted them.
    • Bundle for lunch: pair the can with sandwiches or snacks in meal deals.

    FAQs

    What is the shelf life of the mango beverage with nata de coco?

    The product has a shelf life of up to 24 months from the production date when stored in a cool, dry place.

    Can I change the flavour or the sweetness?

    Yes. Through our OEM and ODM service you can adjust sweetness, choose other fruits such as lychee, pineapple or strawberry, and change the amount of nata de coco.

    What is the minimum order quantity?

    Our MOQ is flexible and can be agreed per project, so you can test a new market before scaling up. Contact our sales team for a quote.
