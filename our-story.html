From formulation to export-ready product delivery

A streamlined manufacturing journey designed for quality and scale — combining product development, advanced production, strict quality control, professional packaging, and reliable export support.

  1. 01

    Raw material inspection

    Only the finest ingredients pass our strict entry checks.

  2. 02

    Material transport to washing

    A controlled line keeps every ingredient clean and fresh.

  3. 03

    Raw material washing

    Multi-stage washing removes impurities and keeps natural flavour.

  4. 04

    Changing area

    Hygiene gates prepare every team member for production.

  5. 05

    Preliminary processing room

    Ingredients are prepped to production-ready standards.

  6. 06

    Filter conveyor

    Continuous filtering protects purity at every transfer.

  7. 07

    Mixing & pasteurisation

    Each recipe is blended to spec, then heat-treated for safety and shelf life.

  8. 08

    Filling & sealing

    Automated lines fill and seal every can and bottle in a closed, hygienic room.

  9. 09

    Incubation area

    Controlled incubation locks in quality and stability before export.

Why choose us

A leading premium beverage manufacturer and supplier in Vietnam. From formulation to final shipment, RITA delivers premium beverages at scale — combining in-house R&D, flexible OEM/ODM production, and export-ready quality for brands worldwide.

  • Free sample

    Receive a customised product sample within 7–14 days to evaluate the flavour, quality, packaging, and overall product direction before moving forward with mass production.

  • Free label design

    Get professional label design support tailored to your brand, product concept, and target market, helping you create attractive packaging ready for production and retail.

  • Small MOQ (1x20ft)

    Start with just one 20ft container to test your market, reduce initial investment, manage inventory more efficiently, and grow your beverage business with flexibility.

  • OEM/ODM & private label

    Bring your own formula or work with our experienced R&D team to develop customised flavours, ingredients, packaging, and beverage concepts for your target market.

  • Fast turnaround

    Move from approved product concept to export-ready mass production in approximately 4–8 weeks, supported by streamlined development, manufacturing, and quality control.

  • Logistics and after-sales services

    From export documentation and container loading to shipping coordination and repeat orders, our team provides reliable support throughout your long-term business partnership.
