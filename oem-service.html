How your beverage is produced

Every batch follows the same controlled route through our factory, from the first ingredient check to stable, export-ready stock.

  1. 01

    Raw material inspection

    Only the finest ingredients pass our strict entry checks.

  2. 02

    Material transport to washing

    A controlled line keeps every ingredient clean and fresh.

  3. 03

    Raw material washing

    Multi-stage washing removes impurities and keeps natural flavour.

  4. 04

    Changing area

    Hygiene gates prepare every team member for production.

  5. 05

    Preliminary processing room

    Ingredients are prepped to production-ready standards.

  6. 06

    Filter conveyor

    Continuous filtering protects purity at every transfer.

  7. 07

    Mixing & pasteurisation

    Each recipe is blended to spec, then heat-treated for safety and shelf life.

  8. 08

    Filling & sealing

    Automated lines fill and seal every can and bottle in a closed, hygienic room.

  9. 09

    Incubation area

    Controlled incubation locks in quality and stability before export.

FAQs

Your own recipe, your own label, your own market: RITA has the lines, the flexibility and the volume to deliver it, anywhere in the world.

Ask a question
What is the minimum order quantity for an OEM run?

MOQ depends on the format you choose. As a rule of thumb we quote one 20ft container per SKU, roughly 24,000 cans or 19,000 PET bottles for the most common sizes. For a first trial we can often work below that, so tell us the volume you have in mind and we will confirm what is feasible.

Can you produce using my own recipe and specifications?

Yes, that is exactly what OEM means at RITA. You bring the formula, the target specification and the brand; our technical team validates it against our equipment, sources the ingredients and runs pilot batches until the result matches your reference sample. If a component is not available locally, we tell you early and propose an equivalent rather than change your product without asking.

Do you provide samples before mass production?

Always. No line is booked until you have tasted and approved a physical sample. We ship pilot samples in the final packaging format so you can assess taste, colour, fill level and label finish together, and we keep iterating until you sign off in writing.

How long does a full OEM order take?

For a product built on an existing formula, expect around 20–25 days from order confirmation to loaded container. A new formula, new packaging tooling or a certification your market requires brings it closer to 4–8 weeks. You receive a dated plan at the quotation stage, not an estimate that moves after you commit.

Can you handle label design and packaging artwork?

Yes. Send finished artwork and we will prepare it for print, or let our design team build the label from your brand guidelines. Either way we check every version against the labelling rules of your destination market, including ingredient declarations, nutrition panels and language requirements, before the plates are made.

Which certifications do your products carry?

Our facilities operate under ISO 22000, HACCP, GMP, BRC Food, FSSC 22000 and HALAL. Every batch ships with its own certificate of analysis, and we can arrange third-party inspection if your buyer or your bank requires it.

Can you assist with export documentation?

We export to more than 180 countries, so the paperwork is routine for us. Certificates of origin, phytosanitary and health certificates, HALAL documentation, packing lists and bills of lading are all prepared in-house. Tell us the destination port and Incoterm and we will confirm exactly which documents apply.
